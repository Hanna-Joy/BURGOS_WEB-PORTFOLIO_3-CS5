## About Me

---

I'm Hanna Joy, a designer based in Manila. I make brand identities, illustrations, and web layouts that feel clean, bright, and easy to trust.

My work starts with a simple idea: people do their best work when they feel confident. I design with that in mind, so every logo, page, and layout helps a brand show up with ease.

When I'm not designing, I'm sketching wireframes by hand, collecting color palettes, and looking for quiet corners with good light.
